Questions about visiting

How do I get to NITA Gallery in Surry Hills?

NITA Gallery is at 68 Campbell Street, Surry Hills, Sydney NSW 2010. The easiest way is by train: Central and Museum stations are both close, and you finish on foot. A map with a Google Maps link is at https://www.nitagallery.com.au/nita-gallery-map.pdf.

Is there parking near NITA Gallery?

Parking around Campbell Street is limited and hard to find, so we suggest catching a train. If you do need to drive, there is a car park right across the road from the gallery.

What are NITA Gallery's opening hours?

NITA Gallery is open Tuesday and Wednesday 10am to 6pm, Thursday 10am to 8pm, Friday 10am to 6pm and Saturday 10am to 3pm. It is closed on Sunday and Monday, but open by appointment any day.

Is NITA Gallery open late?

Yes. NITA Gallery is open until 8pm on Thursday, which is a good evening to visit after work.

Can I visit NITA Gallery outside opening hours?

Yes. NITA Gallery is open by appointment any day of the week, including outside the advertised hours. Call Ritushka on 0405 441 516 or Nick on 0403 835 467, or write to studio@nickbrandphotography.com, and the gallery will be opened for you.

What is there to see at NITA Gallery?

Abstract paintings and limited edition prints by Ritushka, fine-art black-and-white photography by Nick Brand Photography (Fifty Pages of Shades), Nikki B scarves and sarongs printed with photographs of real places, and Second Skin jewellery. Ritushka's painting studio and Nick Brand's photo studio are in the same building.

What other art galleries are near NITA Gallery?

Other Surry Hills galleries within easy reach include STATION, 91 Campbell Street, Surry Hills; Campbell Project Space, 78 Campbell Street, Surry Hills; N.Smith Gallery, 15 Foster Street, Surry Hills; Flinders Street Gallery, 61 Flinders Street, Surry Hills. Opening hours differ, so check each gallery before you go.

Can I buy art at NITA Gallery?

Yes. Paintings, prints, photographs, scarves and jewellery can be bought in the gallery or online at https://www.nitagallery.com.au/shop, with delivery worldwide.
