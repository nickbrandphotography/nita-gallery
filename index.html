The Gallery

Two artists. One room. A great deal of colour.

NITA Gallery is an art gallery at 68 Campbell Street, Surry Hills, Sydney, with a painting studio and a photo studio under one roof: Ritushka is an abstract painter, and Nick Brand Photography is a photographic artist. It is named for the two of us. Inside you'll also find Nick's fashion label Nikki B, with wearable art in the form of silky scarves and sarongs, and a cabinet of jewellery. Choose a door.

Good to know

Questions
and answers.

Where is NITA Gallery?

NITA Gallery is at 68 Campbell Street, Surry Hills, Sydney NSW 2010. Parking nearby is difficult, so catching a train is suggested; if you do need to drive, there is a car park right across the road.

What are NITA Gallery's opening hours?

NITA Gallery is open Tuesday and Wednesday 10am to 6pm, Thursday 10am to 8pm, Friday 10am to 6pm and Saturday 10am to 3pm. It is closed on Sunday and Monday, but the gallery is open by appointment any day, including outside these hours. To arrange a visit, call Ritushka on 0405 441 516 or Nick on 0403 835 467, or write to studio@nickbrandphotography.com.

Can I visit NITA Gallery outside opening hours?

Yes. NITA Gallery is open by appointment any day of the week, including outside the advertised opening hours. Call Ritushka on 0405 441 516 or Nick on 0403 835 467, or write to studio@nickbrandphotography.com, to arrange a time.

Are there studios at NITA Gallery?

Yes. Ritushka runs a painting studio and Nick Brand runs a photo studio at NITA Gallery, 68 Campbell Street, Surry Hills. To ask about either studio, write to studio@nickbrandphotography.com or visit during opening hours.

What can I see at NITA Gallery?

NITA Gallery shows abstract paintings by Ritushka, fine-art black-and-white photography by Nick Brand Photography (the Fifty Pages of Shades series), wearable art in the form of scarves and sarongs from the Nikki B label, and a cabinet of jewellery.

Who runs NITA Gallery?

NITA Gallery was opened by two artists: Ritushka, an abstract painter who runs a painting studio there, and Nick Brand, a Sydney fine-art photographer with more than twenty years' experience who runs a photo studio there. It is named for the two of them.

How much does the art cost?

Prices are in Australian dollars. Ritushka's available original paintings are currently from A$1,450 to A$5,250 framed. Nick Brand Photography's Fifty Pages of Shades limited editions of 15 are priced by the size of print, so please inquire. Nikki B scarves and sarongs are AUD $120 each, and Second Skin jewellery prices are shown on each piece in the shop. Artwork prices are confirmed at enquiry.

Can I buy the artworks, scarves and jewellery?

Yes. Paintings, Fifty Pages of Shades, Second Skin jewellery and Nikki B scarves and sarongs can be bought online in the shop at https://www.nitagallery.com.au/shop, or in the gallery at 68 Campbell Street. For anything priced on enquiry, visit the gallery or write to studio@nickbrandphotography.com.

Does NITA Gallery deliver worldwide?

Yes. The online shop delivers worldwide. The delivery price depends on the country, and you can see it in your cart before you pay. Payment is taken securely by Stripe, in Australian dollars.

Does NITA Gallery sell art prints?

Yes. Ritushka's limited edition prints are sold in the online shop at https://www.nitagallery.com.au/shop. Each is an archival pigment giclee on cotton rag paper, hand-signed and numbered, in sizes from small to large, from AUD $100 unframed, printed to order in Sydney.

How do I contact NITA Gallery?

Email studio@nickbrandphotography.com, use the contact form on this page, or visit 68 Campbell Street, Surry Hills during opening hours.
