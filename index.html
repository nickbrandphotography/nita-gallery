The Gallery

Two artists. One room. A great deal of colour.

NITA is a pop-up gallery for Ritushka, an abstract painter, and Nick Brand, a photographic artist. It is named for the two of us. Inside you'll also find Nick's fashion label Nikki B, with wearable art in the form of silky scarves and sarongs, and a cabinet of jewellery. Choose a door.
