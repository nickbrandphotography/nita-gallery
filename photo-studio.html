Questions

Is there a photo studio at NITA Gallery in Surry Hills?

Yes. NITA Gallery, 68 Campbell Street, Surry Hills, Sydney NSW 2010, has a working photo studio run by Nick Brand Photography. You can book a portrait session there.

Can I book a portrait session at NITA Gallery?

Yes. To book a portrait session with Nick Brand, call Nick on 0403 835 467 or write to studio@nickbrandphotography.com. Tell him what you have in mind and he will reply with how he would approach it, and a time that suits you.

Who is the photographer?

Nick Brand is a Sydney fine-art photographer with more than twenty years' experience as a professional photographer and artist. His website is nickbrandphotography.com.

How much does a portrait session cost?

The price depends on what you need, so please get in touch for a quote. Call Nick on 0403 835 467 or write to studio@nickbrandphotography.com.

When can I visit the photo studio?

The photo studio is inside NITA Gallery, which is open Tuesday and Wednesday 10am to 6pm, Thursday 10am to 8pm, Friday 10am to 6pm and Saturday 10am to 3pm, and by appointment any day, including outside those hours.

Where is the photo studio?

At 68 Campbell Street, Surry Hills, Sydney NSW 2010. Central and Museum stations are close, and there is a car park right across the road. A map is at https://www.nitagallery.com.au/nita-gallery-map.pdf.
