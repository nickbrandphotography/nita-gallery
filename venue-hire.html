Venue hire
in Surry Hills.

Hire NITA Gallery, a working art gallery at 68 Campbell Street, Surry Hills, Sydney, for your next event. It holds up to 40 guests standing, with original paintings and photography on the walls, and costs from $120 an hour. Ideal for Christmas parties, drinks, launches, workshops and photo shoots.

Check a date Call Nick 0403 835 467

The space

Your guests walk into a room that already looks finished. There's no theming and no hired décor, just real art, gallery lighting and a relaxed, intimate atmosphere where people stay and talk. Abstract paintings by Ritushka and black-and-white fine-art photography by Nick Brand stay on the walls for your event.

NITA Gallery is run by the artists themselves. If you'd like, Ritushka or Nick will welcome your guests and give a short talk about the work, at no extra cost. It turns an ordinary drinks function into an evening people remember. Or you can have the gallery to yourselves.

Your guests also get a discount on anything they buy in the gallery during your event: paintings, prints, photographs, Nikki B scarves and Second Skin jewellery.

Questions

Can I hire NITA Gallery for an event?

Yes. NITA Gallery, 68 Campbell Street, Surry Hills, Sydney NSW 2010, is available for exclusive hire for private events, Christmas parties, launches, workshops, meetings and photo or video shoots. Write to studio@nitagallery.com.au or call Nick on 0403 835 467 or Ritushka on 0405 441 516.

How much does it cost to hire NITA Gallery?

Daytime hire is $120 an hour (2 hour minimum). Weeknight evenings are $160 an hour (3 hour minimum), and Friday or Saturday evenings are $200 an hour (4 hour minimum). A 4-hour evening package is $750 Monday to Thursday or $950 Friday or Saturday. A full day is $1,100 on weekdays or $1,400 on weekends. Prices are in Australian dollars.

How many people does NITA Gallery hold?

The gallery is close to 80 square metres and is comfortable for up to 40 guests standing.

Can we have a Christmas party at NITA Gallery?

Yes. The gallery is a good size for small Christmas and end-of-year parties of up to 40 guests. From 1 to 20 December, Thursday to Saturday evenings are charged at 20% above the usual rate, so a Friday or Saturday evening package is $1,140. December dates are limited, so enquire early.

Is catering available?

Yes. Catering can be arranged for your event and is quoted per head. There is no kitchen at the gallery.

Will the artists be there?

If you would like them to be. NITA Gallery is run by the artists Ritushka and Nick Brand, and one of them can welcome your guests and give a short talk about the work at no extra cost. Or you can have the gallery to yourselves.

Can guests buy art during the event?

Yes. The paintings, photographs, scarves and jewellery stay on display, and your guests get a discount on anything they buy in the gallery during your event.

Is NITA Gallery wheelchair accessible?

There is a wheelchair-accessible toilet. Please tell us about any access needs when you enquire.

Can I hire the gallery for a photo shoot or workshop?

Yes. Photo and video shoots, workshops, art classes and meetings are charged at the daytime rate of $120 an hour with a 2 hour minimum. Daytime hire is available on Mondays, Sundays and before 10am on other days.

Where is NITA Gallery and how do guests get there?

At 68 Campbell Street, Surry Hills, Sydney NSW 2010. Central and Museum stations are a short walk away, and there is a car park directly across the road.
