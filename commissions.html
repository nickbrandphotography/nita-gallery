How a commission works

You can also start a brief on Ritushka's commission page, and see framing options and how large works are installed.

  • 1. BriefShare your space, size, palette and timeline. The studio replies within two business days.
  • 2. ProposalReceive a concept, dimensions and a quote, with a 50% deposit to begin.
  • 3. CreationYour painting is created over four to eight weeks, with progress images.

Questions

Does Ritushka take commissions?

Yes. Ritushka paints commissioned abstract paintings in acrylic on canvas for collectors, interior designers, corporate clients, hotels and hospitality venues, made to the size and palette of the space.

How does a painting commission work?

There are four steps. First, share the space, size, palette and timeline in a brief. Second, receive a concept, dimensions and a quote, with a 50% deposit to begin. Third, the painting is created over four to eight weeks, with progress images. Fourth, after final approval and payment of the balance, it is delivered with a certificate of authenticity.

How long does a commissioned painting take?

Usually four to eight weeks from the deposit, with progress images along the way.

Can Ritushka paint for a hotel, restaurant or office?

Yes. Ritushka works with hotels, hospitality venues and corporate clients, as well as private collectors. Large-scale and custom-size works are possible, and any painting can be custom framed to suit the room.

Does Ritushka work with interior designers?

Yes. There is a program for interior designers, art consultants, buyer's agents and corporate clients, with trade pricing, reserved previews, custom sizes and white-glove delivery.

Can commissioned paintings be shipped?

Yes. Paintings are delivered worldwide, fully insured and tracked.

How do I start a commission?

Call Ritushka on 0405 441 516 or write to studio@ritushka.art with your space, size, palette and timeline. You can also see the paintings in person at NITA Gallery, 68 Campbell Street, Surry Hills.
