Exhibitions
in Surry Hills.

NITA Gallery, 68 Campbell Street, Surry Hills, holds exhibitions of abstract painting, fine-art photography and wearable art. Between shows the walls always hang Ritushka's abstract paintings and Nick Brand's Fifty Pages of Shades. Entry is free, Tuesday to Saturday.
